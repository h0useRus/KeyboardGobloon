Keyboard Gobloon English language pack

The data in this pack (words.fst, trigrams.bin) is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
It is separate from Keyboard Gobloon's code (MIT).

Sources:
- Word forms: SCOWL (Spell Checker Oriented Word Lists) 2020.12.07, sizes up to 70, by Kevin
  Atkinson, http://wordlist.aspell.net/ - see LICENSE-SCOWL.txt for its copyright notice.
- Word frequencies: wordfreq 3.1 by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, data under
  CC BY-SA 4.0. wordfreq contains data from OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 (http://opus.nlpl.eu/,
  originating from http://www.opensubtitles.org/), SUBTLEX word lists by Marc Brysbaert et al.
  (freely available data, http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies), Wikipedia,
  ParaCrawl, the Leeds Internet Corpus and Google Books Ngrams (http://books.google.com/ngrams).

Changes: words were lowercased, merged, filtered to the pack's alphabet, and turned into a
frequency map and a letter-trigram model.

Keyboard Gobloon Russian language pack

The data in this pack (words.fst, trigrams.bin) is licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
It is separate from Keyboard Gobloon's code (MIT).

Sources:
- Word forms: the OpenCorpora dictionary, http://opencorpora.org/, licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), as packaged in pymorphy3-dicts-ru.
- Word frequencies: wordfreq 3.1 by Robyn Speer, https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq, data under
  CC BY-SA 4.0. wordfreq contains data from OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 (http://opus.nlpl.eu/,
  originating from http://www.opensubtitles.org/), Wikipedia, ParaCrawl, the Leeds Internet
  Corpus and Google Books Ngrams (http://books.google.com/ngrams).

Changes: every word form was listed, forms with ё were also spelled with е, words were
lowercased, filtered to the pack's alphabet, and turned into a frequency map and a
letter-trigram model.
